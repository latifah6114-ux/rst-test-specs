THE STORY · THE SOURCEP14 · The Story
THE SOURCE — EVERYTHING ELSE WAS ITS SHADOW
⛔ HERE BEGINS THE STORY ⛔

Not all human goals, even when many, are one. Not all elegant covers reflect what they contain. Not all slow, heavy steps — nor the surrender to what is said without thinking, against one's own nature — come from conviction. Sometimes they come because someone saw in the speaker a spark of successful intelligence, and wanted it.

In that exaggerated focus, in those bewildered features, in that surrendered obedience — there is a weight with no measure.

A word she heard took her back to a time that hurt her. A look that resembled another look. Or even an idea that cast her to the bottom.

My reader — what destroyed their internal programming was heavier upon them than material calculation or logical goals.

And what the one who threw an idea shows — imagining he has touched the depth of the unconscious mind, arrogant in what he memorized, condescending in his conclusions — is nothing but an unconscious response from a tangled, exhausted mind. A mind that wants a straw. Wants an idea. Wants an escape from itself.

And it was done.

When you spoke about perfection, every sigh and every smile from you carved its way to me truthfully — and I am certain it reached everyone the same way. And at the bottom — where everyone wished her — there she was, in her apparent comfort that covered pyramids of helplessness, provoking with strange contradictions, gasping for the straw…

Those feelings were the same. Or perhaps they renewed themselves, and awakened a monstrous, mined flow of shock.

I did not truly realize there was a continuous pain — in presence and absence, in waking and sleep. A pain that changed colors and shapes, the taste of every delicious thing, the smell of everything that once pleased — until even the air had the smell of pain.

THE STORY · THE SOURCEP15 · The Story (cont.)
THE SOURCE — EVERYTHING ELSE WAS ITS SHADOW

The idea of perfection had a pictorial presence in my imagination. And when you said there is no perfection except His Face, glory be to Him — I found that we are all human. Everyone in the orchestrated show — all human. I too am human.

By what scale are they weighed? Who led them? How did the image flow? How was the judgment announced against me, issued, agreed upon, executed — it is nothing but human minds. I cannot and dare not classify them. Because they are simply human. And because there is no perfection except for God alone.

Some may see arrogance in my description. Some may sense a reply in it. But believe me — this is truly me. Yes. The human who cannot reach perfection. The one full of a neural network governed by precise centres in the brain, which can be damaged in a second by a shock from a human the size and weight of a straw.

I lifted him. I valued him. He betrayed, belittled, underestimated.

But with all honesty, after all experiences and levels of pain — I feel pity for those for whom it is easy to defame or hurt a person who may regret hurting someone who does not deserve to be hurt. How does he continue as if nothing happened? How does he sleep peacefully and enjoy the company of his loved ones truthfully — while someone else was exhausted because of him?

I could not get over these feelings after hearing that tone in the layers of your voice — delivering the elegant philosophy of perfection and the pleasure in it. What you mean may be completely different. But it widened my horizons and took me to a sharp angle where I sensed its details and found in it part of that pleasure in the heart of painful feelings — for transcendence, elevation, rising of thought.

An understanding that is not instinct — but the depth of intellectual space, faith in destiny, and rising above translating intentions and translating what lies behind reactions or spontaneous positivity. My leader, whom I am proud to be a student in your ranks — your honest words crystallized the idea and completed the cycle of its comprehension and its practice, consciously.

Without injustice to yourself, nor cancellation of it. Choosing what suits its peace and its status. And acknowledging that you may one day prove that you are only human — just as they proved in their arena, in their place, under their control, their judgment, and their perception.

— Latifah Almutairi · 5 September 2026 —
